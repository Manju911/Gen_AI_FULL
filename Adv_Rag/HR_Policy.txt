Company HR Policy

Employees are entitled to 20 paid leaves every calendar year.

Leave Types:
- Casual Leave: 10 days
- Sick Leave: 8 days
- Earned Leave: 2 days

Employees must apply for leave through the HR portal at least one day in advance.

Unused earned leave can be carried forward according to company policy.

Working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
