# Mysore Trip Plan: A Complete 2–3 Day Itinerary

Mysore, officially Mysuru, is one of Karnataka’s most charming cities, known for its royal heritage, grand palaces, temples, gardens, silk, sandalwood, and excellent South Indian food. A **2-day trip** is ideal for first-time visitors, while **3 days** allows you to add nearby historical and nature spots.

---

## Best Time to Visit Mysore

### Ideal season: **October to March**
This is the best time for sightseeing because the weather is pleasant and comfortable.

### Special time to visit: **Mysuru Dasara**
If you want to see Mysore at its grandest, visit during the **Dasara festival**, usually in September or October. The palace, processions, cultural events, and city decorations are spectacular. However, hotels and transport get booked early, so plan well in advance.

### Avoid if possible
April to June can be hot, especially during afternoons. If visiting in summer, plan outdoor sightseeing early in the morning or late evening.

---

## Recommended Trip Duration

### 1 Day
Good for a quick trip from Bengaluru, covering Mysore Palace, Chamundi Hills, and maybe Brindavan Gardens.

### 2 Days
Best for a comfortable first-time Mysore trip. You can cover the main city attractions without rushing.

### 3 Days
Ideal if you want to include nearby places like **Srirangapatna**, **Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary**, **Somnathpur Temple**, or **Nanjangud**.

---

# How to Reach Mysore

## From Bengaluru

### By Train
One of the easiest and most comfortable ways to reach Mysore. Trains are frequent, affordable, and usually convenient for tourists.

### By Bus
KSRTC and private buses operate regularly between Bengaluru and Mysore. This is a good budget option.

### By Car or Taxi
The road journey from Bengaluru to Mysore usually takes around **3–4 hours**, depending on traffic. This is a good option if you want flexibility and want to stop on the way.

### Popular stop on the Bengaluru–Mysore route
You can try **Maddur vada** at Maddur or stop for breakfast/lunch along the highway.

---

## By Air

Mysore has a small airport with limited flights. Most travelers fly to **Bengaluru Airport** and then take a train, bus, or cab to Mysore.

# Where to Stay in Mysore

## Best areas for tourists

### Near Mysore Palace
Best for first-time visitors. You will be close to the main attractions, markets, restaurants, and transport options.

### Near Railway Station
Good if you are arriving by train and want easy connectivity.

# Suggested 2-Day Mysore Itinerary

## Day 1: Mysore Palace, Zoo, Market, and Palace Lighting

### Morning: Mysore Palace

Start your trip with the most famous attraction in the city: **Mysore Palace**.

It is one of India’s grandest royal palaces and a must-visit for anyone coming to Mysore. The architecture, royal halls, paintings, courtyards, and intricate interiors give you a strong sense of the city’s royal past.

**Suggested time:** 1.5 to 2 hours

### Late Morning or Afternoon: Mysore Zoo

Next, visit **Mysore Zoo**, one of the oldest and most well-maintained zoos in India. It is a great place for families, children, and wildlife lovers.

If you are not interested in visiting the zoo, you can choose **Jaganmohan Palace Art Gallery** instead. It is a good option for those interested in paintings, art, and royal collections.

### Lunch

Try a traditional South Indian meal. You can have:

- Mysore masala dosa  
- Idli-vada  
- South Indian thali  
- Bisi bele bath  
- Filter coffee  

### Evening: Devaraja Market

Visit **Devaraja Market** in the evening. It is one of the most colorful places in Mysore and is great for experiencing local life.

You can find:

- Flowers  
- Fruits  
- Spices  
- Incense sticks  
- Sandalwood products  
- Local snacks  
- Traditional items  

This is also a good place for photography and casual shopping.

### Night: Mysore Palace Illumination

If available during your visit, do not miss the **Mysore Palace lighting**. The illuminated palace looks beautiful and is one of the most memorable sights in Mysore.

Make sure to check the current lighting schedule before planning your evening.

---

## Day 2: Chamundi Hills, St. Philomena’s Church, Shopping, and Brindavan Gardens

### Morning: Chamundi Hills and Chamundeshwari Temple

Begin your day with a visit to **Chamundi Hills**, one of the most important religious and scenic spots in Mysore.

Visit the **Chamundeshwari Temple**, dedicated to Goddess Chamundeshwari. The hill also offers lovely views of Mysore city.

On the way, stop to see the large **Nandi statue**, another popular landmark.

**Tip:** Visit early in the morning to avoid crowds and heat.

### Late Morning: St. Philomena’s Church

After Chamundi Hills, visit **St. Philomena’s Church**, a beautiful Gothic-style church and one of Mysore’s most famous landmarks.

Its tall spires, stained-glass windows, and peaceful interiors make it worth a visit.

### Lunch

Return to the city for lunch. Try local favorites such as:

- Mysore masala dosa  
- Set dosa  
- South Indian meals  
- Filter coffee  
- Mysore Pak  

### Afternoon: Shopping and Relaxation

Spend the afternoon shopping or relaxing at your hotel.

Good things to buy in Mysore include:

- Mysore Pak  
- Sandalwood soaps and oils  
- Incense sticks  
- Mysore silk sarees  
- Coffee powder  
- Wooden handicrafts  
- Local spices  

Popular shopping areas include:

- Devaraja Market  
- Sayyaji Rao Road  
- Government handicraft shops  
- Cauvery Emporium-type stores  
- Local sweet shops for Mysore Pak  

### Evening: Brindavan Gardens

In the evening, visit **Brindavan Gardens**, located near the Krishna Raja Sagar Dam, a little outside Mysore city.

The gardens are known for landscaped lawns, fountains, and the musical fountain show. It is best visited in the evening when the lights are on.

**Tip:** Hire a cab or auto arrangement for this visit because it is outside the main city.

# Optional Day 3 Itinerary

If you have an extra day, choose one of the following routes.

---

## Option 1: Srirangapatna and Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary

This is the best option if you enjoy history and nature.

### Places to visit

#### Srirangapatna
A historic town closely associated with Tipu Sultan.

#### Daria Daulat Bagh
Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace, known for its paintings and Indo-Islamic style.

#### Gumbaz
The mausoleum of Tipu Sultan and his family.

#### Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary
A peaceful bird sanctuary, ideal for nature lovers and photography. Boating may be available depending on conditions.

This route is good for families, history lovers, and birdwatchers.

---

## Option 2: Somnathpur Temple

Choose this option if you love architecture and heritage.

Visit the **Chennakesava Temple at Somnathpur**, famous for its detailed Hoysala-style carvings. It is one of the most beautiful temple sites near Mysore.

This is a great half-day or relaxed full-day trip.

---

## Option 3: Nanjangud

This is a good choice if you are interested in temples and religious places.

Visit the famous **Srikanteshwara Temple** at Nanjangud, an important pilgrimage site near Mysore.

---

# Local Transport in Mysore

## Auto-rickshaws
Good for short distances within the city. Confirm the fare before starting or ask them to use the meter where possible.

## App-based cabs
Available in many areas and useful for comfortable city travel.

## Private cab or full-day taxi
Best for covering Chamundi Hills, Brindavan Gardens, Srirangapatna, Ranganathittu, or Somnathpur.

## Local buses
A budget-friendly option, though less convenient if you are on a short trip.

### Best recommendation
For a family or group, hire a **full-day cab** for Day 2 or Day 3. For solo or budget travelers, use a mix of autos, buses, and app-based cabs.

# Important Travel Tips

1. **Check timings in advance** for Mysore Palace, Zoo, Brindavan Gardens, and temples.  
2. **Book hotels early** if traveling during weekends, holidays, or Dasara.  
3. **Start early** for Chamundi Hills to avoid crowds.  
4. **Keep cash** for markets, autos, small shops, and local snacks.  
5. **Dress modestly** when visiting temples.  
6. **Plan Brindavan Gardens in the evening** for the best experience.  
7. **Hire a cab** if traveling with family or if you want a relaxed trip.  
8. **Do not overpack the itinerary**; Mysore is best enjoyed at a comfortable pace.  

---

# Final Recommendation

For a first-time trip to Mysore, plan a **2-day visit**. Stay near Mysore Palace, Nazarbad, or the railway station for convenience.

A balanced itinerary would be:

**Day 1:** Mysore Palace, Zoo or Jaganmohan Palace, Devaraja Market, Palace lighting  
**Day 2:** Chamundi Hills, St. Philomena’s Church, local shopping, Brindavan Gardens  

If you can extend to **3 days**, add **Srirangapatna and Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary** or **Somnathpur Temple** for a richer heritage and nature experience.
